# Respondents are never named to the client, on any engagement

Anonymity is a standing term of every research engagement, not a per-project courtesy that can
be traded away when a client asks. It is the reason practitioners speak candidly about
competitors' pricing and their own vendor complaints, and one named respondent list is enough
to make the next twenty interviews useless — nobody gives a real answer once they know it
travels back with their name on it. When a client wants the list for their sales team, the
answer is a counter-offer, not a refusal: segment and company-size tags per respondent
("mid-market, 200–500 employees") deliver the context they actually want without a name
attached. Like [[hold-the-fee-negotiate-scope]], the move is to trade the shape of the
deliverable rather than the term that protects the work.

**Source:** Northwind Labs' GTM lead asked for a named respondent list on the 2026-08-14 call;
declined, compromise offered and accepted. The same ask has come up on earlier engagements —
this is the pattern, not the incident.
