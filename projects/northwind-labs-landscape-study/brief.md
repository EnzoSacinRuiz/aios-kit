# Northwind Labs — category landscape study

## Client

Northwind Labs — B2B software, ~180 people, expanding their platform into a new vertical.
See `knowledge/external/northwind-labs-pushes-for-rush-timelines.md` for the standing account
note on how they negotiate.

## Objective

Map the competitive landscape in the new vertical Northwind is entering: who else is playing
there, how they position, and where the gaps are — enough for Northwind's leadership to
decide whether to build a dedicated feature set or partner instead.

## Scope

- 8 practitioner interviews with buyers and users currently in the category.
- Desk research across 12 identified competitors: positioning, pricing tier, and public
  customer complaints.
- One synthesis workshop with Northwind's product and GTM leads to pressure-test findings
  before the report is finalized.

## Timeline

- Week 1: interview guide + competitor shortlist, reviewed by Ana.
- Weeks 2–3: interviews (Tomas leads scheduling and first-pass notes, Iris runs half the
  calls).
- Week 4: synthesis workshop + draft report.
- Week 5: final report delivered.
