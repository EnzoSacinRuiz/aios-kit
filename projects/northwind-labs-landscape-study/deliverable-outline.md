# Deliverable outline — Northwind Labs landscape study

1. **Executive summary** — one page, three findings, one recommendation.
2. **Category map** — the 12 competitors plotted by positioning and pricing tier.
3. **Buyer language** — what practitioners actually call the problem, in their own words,
   pulled from interview transcripts.
4. **Gap analysis** — where every mapped competitor falls short, and which gaps are big
   enough to build against.
5. **Recommendation** — build, partner, or wait, with the reasoning tied back to sections 2–4.
6. **Appendix** — interview guide, respondent list (anonymized), full transcript index.

Format: slide deck for the workshop, written report for the final delivery. Both draft from
the same synthesis doc — never written twice from scratch.
