# Hold the fee, negotiate scope instead

When a prospect or client pushes on price or timeline, the answer is never a discount or a
rush surcharge — it's a scope trade-off: fewer interviews, a narrower category, a lighter
synthesis. This came out of dropping quick-read engagements that ate a full week for a fee
that didn't cover the hours, and it shows up again with Northwind Labs' recurring rush
requests and with prospects who compare us to Pulse's lower price. Same principle, three
different triggers.
